Our story

Founded in 1997, we at UBM are a dedicated group of pro AV professionals, system designers and technology managers working together to accelerate the adoption of the right AV solutions, with one goal: to serve your AV distribution needs.

Through interoperability and standardisation, the products we distribute enable great AV experiences. UBM can design and implement professional AV solutions that are cost-effective, agile and scalable, meeting the needs of today and tomorrow.

Our greatest strength is our knowledgeable, helpful team. Through confident, experienced people we help our dealer partners navigate unexpected developments and realise important opportunities. Key members of our team have been with us since our humble beginnings, and we build on that as we grow.

1997

UBM founded in Mumbai as a specialist AV distributor.

Growth

Portfolio expands across projection, displays, professional audio and signal management.

Value-added

Design, consultation, installation, maintenance, service and training added to support partners end-to-end.

Today

Serving dealers, integrators and organisations across India with 7 global brands.
