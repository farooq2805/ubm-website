Auditorium AV & Sound

Auditorium sound and AV, engineered for every seat

Professional loudspeakers, mixing, stage microphones, large-format projection and central control, so every speech, performance and event lands perfectly.

An auditorium AV system combines professional loudspeakers and amplifiers, a mixing console and stage boxes, wired and wireless microphones, large-format projection or LED screens, wireless presentation and a central control system for audio, video and lighting. UBM designs and installs auditorium sound and AV systems for educational institutions, corporates, places of worship and community venues. UBM has served customers from Mumbai since 1997.

  • High-brightness laser projectors and LED screens
  • Podium and control-room integration
  • Central control of audio, video and lighting

Coverage before wattage

Great auditorium sound is about even coverage and intelligibility, not just loudness. We model loudspeaker placement for the room so the last row hears as clearly as the first, and choose amplification to match.

Simple for the people who run events

Most auditoriums are operated by staff who are not sound engineers. We design presets and a simple control interface so a lecture, a concert and a prize-giving can each start at the touch of a button.

Frequently asked questions

What does an auditorium sound system include?

An auditorium sound system typically includes loudspeakers, amplifiers, a mixing console, stage boxes, wired and wireless microphones, signal processing and cabling, often integrated with projection and a central control system.

How long does auditorium AV installation take?

Timelines depend on venue size and civil work. After design approval, many auditorium installations are completed in a few weeks. UBM shares a detailed schedule with every proposal.

Do you provide maintenance after installation?

Yes. UBM offers preventive maintenance and service support so your auditorium stays event-ready.
