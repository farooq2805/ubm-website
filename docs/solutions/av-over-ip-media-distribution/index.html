AV over IP & Media Distribution

Route any source to any screen, at any scale

Why networked AV

Traditional matrix switchers have fixed input and output counts. AV over IP scales with your network, so adding a screen is as simple as adding a decoder. It is ideal for campuses, hotels, stadiums and buildings with many displays.

Centralised control, smaller teams

Networked AV allows centralised control of sound, projection, displays and conferencing, letting smaller teams run more rooms and events with confidence.

Frequently asked questions

What is AV over IP?

AV over IP is a method of transmitting audio, video and control signals over a standard IP network, allowing any source to be routed to any display and making systems easy to scale.

When should I choose an HDMI matrix instead of AV over IP?

An HDMI matrix suits a single room or a small, fixed number of sources and displays. AV over IP is better for larger, distributed systems that need to grow over time.

Do you supply ATEN KVM switches?

Yes. UBM supplies ATEN KVM, USB, cable and professional AV products for dealers, system integrators and enterprises.
