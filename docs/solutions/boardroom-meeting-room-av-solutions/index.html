Boardroom & Meeting Room AV

Boardrooms and meeting rooms that just work

One-touch meetings, crystal-clear conferencing audio and wireless sharing, from compact huddle rooms to flagship boardrooms.

Get a free consultationWhatsApp an expert

Boardroom AV is the combination of displays, video conferencing cameras, microphones and speakers, wireless presentation and room control that lets teams meet and collaborate without friction. UBM designs and installs boardroom, conference room and huddle room AV for corporates across India, using Bose Professional conferencing audio, ATEN and Liberty AV signal management, and BenQ, ViewSonic and Epson displays. UBM has served customers from Mumbai since 1997.

What's included

  • Large-format displays, projectors and video walls
  • Video conferencing cameras and soundbars (Teams/Zoom ready)
  • Ceiling and table microphones, conferencing loudspeakers
  • Wireless presentation (BYOD) and HDMI table connectivity
  • Matrix switching, extenders and control systems
  • Room booking and huddle-space standardisation

Meetings without the five-minute fumble

The best boardroom is the one nobody has to think about. We design rooms around a single, simple workflow: walk in, share wirelessly or join the call, and start. Behind that simplicity is careful audio design, clean signal routing and dependable hardware.

Standardise once, roll out everywhere

For organisations with many rooms, we create room standards, small, medium and large, so every meeting space behaves the same way. That makes support easier and roll-outs faster across offices and cities.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a boardroom AV setup?

A boardroom AV setup usually includes a display or projector, a video conferencing camera, microphones and speakers, wireless presentation, switching and cabling, and a control interface. UBM designs the complete system around your room size and meeting style.

Do your systems work with Microsoft Teams and Zoom?

Yes. We specify conferencing hardware that works with the platforms your organisation already uses, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Google Meet.

Can you upgrade our existing meeting rooms?

Yes. We audit existing rooms, reuse what is still good and upgrade what is holding meetings back, often audio and wireless sharing first.
