Public Address & Portable PA

Public address and portable PA, clear when it matters

Announcements, background music and emergency paging, zoned, intelligible and built on proven TOA and Bose Professional hardware.

A public address (PA) system delivers announcements, background music and emergency messages through amplifiers, microphones and zoned loudspeakers across a building or campus. UBM designs, supplies and installs PA and portable PA systems for offices, factories, schools, hotels, retail and places of worship, using TOA and Bose Professional products. Frequently asked questions

What is a PA system used for in an office?

In an office, a PA system is used for announcements, background music and emergency evacuation messages, usually divided into zones such as floors, cafeterias and reception.

Which PA system brands does UBM supply?

UBM supplies TOA and Bose Professional public address and portable PA solutions.

Can a PA system be integrated with fire alarms?

Yes. PA systems can be designed to integrate with emergency and evacuation systems. UBM will advise on the right approach for your building.
