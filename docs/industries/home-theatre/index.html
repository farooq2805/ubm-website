What goes wrong

The problems we hear from home theatre teams

Real questions and complaints from buyers on Reddit, Quora and in our own site visits.

Planned after the interiors

No conduits, no speaker positions and no ceiling mount, so everything ends up on show.

Washed-out picture

Living rooms with big windows and light walls kill projector contrast.

Booming bass, muddy dialogue

Bare walls and hard floors make dialogue hard to follow at sensible volumes.

Power cuts and dust

Projectors and amplifiers need surge protection, backup and ventilation in Indian conditions.

Frequently asked questions

When should I plan my home theatre?

Before interiors are finished, so cables, mounts and speaker positions can be concealed.

Projector or TV for my home theatre?

A projector gives a far bigger picture for the money if light can be controlled; a large TV suits bright living rooms.

Do you work with interior designers and architects?

Yes. We coordinate with your architect and interior designer from planning to handover.

Projector or large TV for a home theatre?

Projectors deliver a far larger, cinema-like image; large displays suit brighter rooms. We recommend based on your space.
