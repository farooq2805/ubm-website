What goes wrong

The problems we hear from retail teams

Real questions and complaints from buyers on Reddit, Quora and in our own site visits.

USB-stick signage

Content updated by hand, store by store, means old offers stay on screen for weeks.

Screens that disappear in daylight

Consumer TVs in shop windows are too dim to compete with Indian sunlight.

Music that doesn't fit

One volume for the whole store: too loud at the till, too quiet in the fitting rooms.

Displays that fail early

Consumer screens running 14 hours a day are not built for retail duty.
