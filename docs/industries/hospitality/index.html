What goes wrong

The problems we hear from hospitality teams

Real questions and complaints from buyers on Reddit, Quora and in our own site visits.

Speeches nobody can hear

Two big speakers at the stage end of a long hall: deafening at the front, mumbling at the back.

Feedback and dropouts

Cheap wireless microphones squeal and cut out, right during the toast.

Every DJ brings their own rig

Without proper input plates and a house system, every event means cables across the floor.

Partitions break the sound

When a ballroom is split into two halls, music and announcements leak from one event into the other.

How UBM fixes it

What changes when UBM designs your space

1

Even coverage, every table

Distributed or column speakers designed so every guest hears clearly, without blasting the front rows.

2

Reliable wireless microphones

Professional handheld and lapel microphones with proper frequency planning.

3

Plug-and-play for DJs and bands

Input plates around the hall feeding a house system the venue controls.

4

Divisible-room presets

One button switches the ballroom between one big event and two separate ones.

Frequently asked questions

What sound system does a banquet hall need?

A distributed or column speaker system designed for even coverage, professional wireless microphones, input plates for DJs and bands, and simple presets for staff.

Can you work around our booking calendar?

Yes. Installation is scheduled between bookings and before peak wedding dates.

Can banquet halls be split into smaller zones?

Yes. We design divisible-room audio and video so a ballroom can operate as one space or several.

Do you supply background music systems?

Yes. We supply and install zoned background music for restaurants, lobbies, spas and outdoor areas.
