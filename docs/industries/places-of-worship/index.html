Places of Worship

Every word heard. Every gathering included.

Speech-clear audio, projection and streaming-ready systems designed around unique architecture and acoustics.

Get a free consultationWhatsApp an expert

India has more places of worship than schools and colleges combined. Each one is acoustically unique: stone mandapams that ring for seconds, domes that swallow speech, and courtyards where thousands gather on festival days. Most still rely on a single amplifier and a pair of horn speakers that are too loud near the entrance and inaudible in the hall. At the same time, noise rules mean loudspeakers must be controlled after 10 pm. UBM helps trusts and committees get every word heard inside, without disturbing the neighbourhood outside.

What goes wrong

The problems we hear from places of worship teams

Real questions and complaints from buyers on Reddit, Quora and in our own site visits.

Echo swallows the words

Stone and marble halls reverberate so much that pravachans, kirtans and sermons become hard to follow.

Loud outside, quiet inside

Horn speakers on the roof reach the street but not the back of the hall, which also invites noise complaints.

Festival-day crowds

Daily systems can't cope when thousands gather for Navratri, Eid, Christmas or Gurpurab.

Volunteers run the system

Committees rely on volunteers, so complicated mixers lead to feedback and mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

Why is sound unclear in temples, churches and mosques?

Hard stone and marble surfaces and high ceilings cause echo. Directional speakers placed correctly make speech clear.

Can the system respect noise regulations?

Yes. Indoor sound can be prioritised and outdoor zones scheduled or limited in line with applicable rules.

Can installation preserve the architecture?

Yes. We choose discreet speakers and cable routes that respect the building.

Why is sound difficult in places of worship?

High ceilings and hard surfaces cause echo. Correct loudspeaker selection and placement is essential for clear speech.

Can you support us through our dealer or consultant?

Yes. We work directly or alongside your consultant, integrator or dealer.
