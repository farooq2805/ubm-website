Hotel Banquet & Ballroom AV Checklist

Planning AV for a hotel ballroom or banquet hall? A checklist covering sound, projection, LED walls, divisible rooms, background music and staff-friendly control.

By the UBM Solutions Team · Updated 2026-09-24

Hotel ballrooms need even, intelligible sound, bright projection or LED screens, divisible-room audio and video, zoned background music and a control system simple enough for banquet staff. This guide is from UBM (Universal Business Machines), a Mumbai-based pro AV distributor since 1997.

Weddings, conferences, product launches and gala dinners all happen in the same ballroom. The AV system has to handle every one of them, operated by banquet staff rather than engineers.

The checklist

  • Even sound coverage across the whole hall, including when it is divided.
  • Microphones, wireless handheld, lapel and lectern options.
  • Projection or LED, bright enough for daytime conferences.
  • Divisible rooms, audio and video that follow partition walls automatically or with one button.
  • Background music zones for pre-function areas.
  • Input plates around the room for bands, DJs and presenters.
  • Simple control panels with presets for common event types.
  • Video distribution to overflow rooms and foyers.

> Design for the staff who will run it: presets and clear labelling prevent most event-day problems.

FAQs

What sound system is best for a banquet hall?

A distributed or zoned system designed for even coverage and speech clarity, with wireless microphones and simple presets.

Can a ballroom AV system handle divided rooms?

Yes. Divisible-room design lets audio and video operate as one large space or several smaller ones.
