Home Theatre Setup in India: Projector, Screen and Sound Planning Guide

Planning a home theatre in an Indian apartment or villa? Room size, projector vs TV, screen size, surround sound, acoustics, power backup and budget planning explained.

By the UBM Solutions Team · Updated 2026-10-18

Quick answer

Plan a home theatre in India around the room first: size, light control and acoustics. Then choose a projector or large TV, a screen sized to seating distance, a surround system matched to the room, and plan power backup and cable routing before interiors are finished. This guide is from UBM (Universal Business Machines), a Mumbai-based pro AV distributor since 1997.

"Creating a home theatre?" threads on r/india and r/hometheater come up again and again, usually from people in apartments where the living room doubles as the theatre. Here is a planning order that avoids expensive mistakes.

1. Start with the room

  • Dedicated room or living room? Dedicated rooms allow full light control and acoustic treatment.
  • Light control. Blackout curtains make a huge difference for projectors.
  • Acoustics. Hard floors and bare walls echo. Rugs, curtains and acoustic panels help.

2. Projector or large TV?

  • Projector: a cinema-sized image (100 to 150 inches) for less money per inch. Best with light control.
  • Large TV: better in bright living rooms, simpler to install, but limited in size.

3. Size the screen to your seating

A comfortable rule is a seating distance of about 1.2 to 1.5 times the screen width for an immersive picture.

4. Choose sound for the room

  • 5.1 is a strong starting point for most Indian living rooms.
  • 7.1 or Atmos suits dedicated rooms with space for rear and height speakers.
  • Position matters more than wattage.

5. Plan for Indian conditions

  • Power: use surge protection and plan inverter or UPS support for the projector to shut down safely.
  • Dust and humidity: choose sealed or filter-free projectors where possible and plan ventilation.
  • Cabling: conceal HDMI and speaker cables before interiors are finished.

> Involve your AV partner before the carpenter. Cable routes, ceiling mounts and speaker positions are far cheaper to plan than to retrofit.

FAQs

Is a projector better than a TV for a home theatre in India?

A projector gives a much larger image for the money and suits rooms with light control. A large TV is better for bright living rooms.

What size screen do I need for my home theatre?

Sit about 1.2 to 1.5 times the screen width away for an immersive picture.

Do I need power backup for a home theatre projector?

It is recommended in India so the projector can cool down and shut down safely during a power cut.
