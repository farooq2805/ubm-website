How to Choose a Pro AV Distributor in India: A Guide for Dealers & Integrators

What separates a value-added AV distributor from a box-mover? Brand portfolio, pre-sales design, project support, stock, credit, training and after-sales, a checklist for dealers and integrators.

By the UBM Solutions Team · Updated 2026-09-02

Pick a distributor for the support behind the products: pre-sales design help, project registration and pricing support, demo access, training, dependable stock and responsive after-sales service. This guide is from UBM (Universal Business Machines), a Mumbai-based pro AV distributor since 1997.

For AV dealers and system integrators, the right distributor is a growth partner. The wrong one is just a price list. Here is what to look for.

1. A complementary brand portfolio

Can you build a complete system, displays, projection, audio, signal management and connectivity, from one partner? Fewer vendors means simpler logistics and one accountable point of contact.

2. Pre-sales design support

Value-added distributors help you design systems, prepare BOQs and choose the right model for the room. That support wins tenders and avoids costly mistakes on site.

3. Project support

Look for help with project pricing, specifications for consultants, and demos for your end customers.

4. Training

Product and system training keeps your team current and confident to sell and install new technology.

5. Stock, logistics and commercial terms

Reliable availability, clear lead times and fair commercial terms protect your margins and your reputation.

6. After-sales service

When something fails, how quickly can your distributor help with replacements, warranty coordination and technical troubleshooting?

A distributor's real value shows up after the order: on site, under deadline, when something needs solving.
