Why Indian Schools Are Switching from Smart Boards to Interactive Flat Panels

Schools across India are replacing projector-based smart boards with interactive flat panels. Here is why, what it costs to switch well, and when a projector still makes sense.

By the UBM Solutions Team · Updated 2026-10-20

Indian schools are moving to interactive flat panels because they are bright in daylight, need no lamp or calibration, start instantly and are easier for teachers to use. Projectors still make sense for large halls and tight per-room budgets. This guide is from UBM (Universal Business Machines), a Mumbai-based pro AV distributor since 1997.

"Why are schools switching to interactive flat panels instead of smart boards?" is one of the most asked education-technology questions on Quora from Indian schools. Teachers on Reddit add a sharper version: "we bought smart boards, so why does nobody use them?"

What changed

The first wave of smart classrooms in India used a projector pointed at an interactive whiteboard. They worked, but they needed dim rooms, regular lamp replacement and calibration, and the teacher's shadow fell across the board. Many ended up used as expensive screens.

Why interactive flat panels are winning

  • Bright in Indian daylight. Most classrooms have large windows and no blackout. Panels stay readable.
  • No lamps, no calibration. Fewer service calls for the school's admin team.
  • Instant on. Teachers start in seconds, which is what decides daily use.
  • Built-in software and wireless sharing. Annotation, whiteboarding and screen sharing without extra boxes.
  • Long life. Panels are designed for years of daily classroom use.

When a projector is still the better buy

  • Lecture halls and auditoriums where a 75 or 86-inch panel is too small for the last row.
  • Budget-sensitive roll-outs across many rooms where image size matters more than touch.
  • Rooms where light can be controlled. Modern laser projectors avoid lamp costs.

The best device is the one teachers use every day. Pick for daylight, simplicity and training, not just screen size.

What makes a smart class roll-out succeed in India

  • Size the display for the back row, not the front.
  • Add audio for classes over 40 students.
  • Train teachers properly and nominate a champion in each school.
  • Choose a supplier with local service, so a fault is fixed in days, not weeks.

FAQs

Are interactive flat panels better than smart boards for Indian schools?

For most classrooms, yes. They stay visible in daylight, need no lamps or calibration and start instantly, which increases daily use by teachers.

Why are smart boards not used much in some schools?

Common reasons are dim or washed-out images, slow start-up, calibration problems and a lack of teacher training. Interactive flat panels and proper training solve most of these.

What size interactive panel does an Indian classroom need?

Most classrooms of 30 to 45 students use 75 or 86-inch panels. Larger rooms may need a projector for a bigger image.
