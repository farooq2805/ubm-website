How to Set Up Video Conferencing in a Meeting Room: A Practical Guide for Indian Offices

Step-by-step guide to setting up Microsoft Teams or Zoom video conferencing in meeting rooms in Indian offices: camera, microphones, display, network and room acoustics.

By the UBM Solutions Team · Updated 2026-10-16

To set up video conferencing in a meeting room, pick a platform (Teams, Zoom or Meet), size the camera and microphones to the room, add a display sized for the far seat, ensure a wired network connection, and treat echo. Standardise the setup across rooms. This guide is from UBM (Universal Business Machines), a Mumbai-based pro AV distributor since 1997.

"How can I set up video conferencing in a meeting room?" is a top Quora question from Indian businesses, and r/sysadmin is full of IT managers asking the same thing. Here is a practical sequence.

Step 1: Choose the platform

Use the platform your company already runs on: Microsoft Teams, Zoom or Google Meet. Certified room systems make joining a call one tap.

Step 2: Match equipment to room size

  • Huddle room (2 to 6 people): an all-in-one video bar and a 55 to 65-inch display.
  • Meeting room (6 to 12): a dedicated camera, ceiling or table microphones and a 75-inch display.
  • Boardroom (12+): auto-framing or speaker-tracking cameras, ceiling microphones, DSP and dual displays.

Step 3: Get audio right

Remote participants judge a meeting by the audio. Microphones must cover every seat, and speakers should be clear without echo.

Step 4: Network and power

  • Use a wired connection for room systems.
  • Agree bandwidth and firewall rules with IT.
  • Put equipment on UPS to ride through power fluctuations.

Step 5: Fix the room acoustics

Glass walls are popular in Indian offices but cause echo. Soft furnishings or acoustic panels help microphones work properly.

Standardise small, medium and large room designs so every meeting room in every city works the same way.

Step 6: Train and support

Show staff how to start, share and end a call, and agree a support process for when a meeting is about to start.

FAQs

What equipment do I need for video conferencing in a meeting room?

A camera, microphones and speakers sized to the room, a display, wireless or wired sharing, and a room system for Teams, Zoom or Meet.

Should conference room systems use Wi-Fi?

A wired network connection is recommended for reliability and call quality.
